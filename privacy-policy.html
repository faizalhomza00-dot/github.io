Privacy Policy — Mizanatik

Last updated: September 2026

Mizanatik is a personal finance application designed to help users manage income, expenses, financial files, transactions, reports, balances, and backups.

Information We Store

Mizanatik stores financial information entered by the user, such as income, expenses, transaction descriptions, amounts, dates, and financial file information. This information is primarily stored locally on the user's device.

Google Drive Backup

Mizanatik provides an optional Google Drive backup feature. When the user chooses to use Google Drive backup, the application uses the user's Google account to create, update, or restore the application's backup file.

Mizanatik uses the Google Drive permission required to manage files created or used by the application. Mizanatik does not request access to all files in the user's Google Drive.

Personal Information

Mizanatik does not sell, rent, or share users' personal information with third parties for advertising purposes.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

User Control

Users can choose whether to use Google Drive backup. Users can also remove their backup data from Google Drive through their Google Drive account.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
